# Probability Distributions & Aptitude Ratios

Section: General Aptitude
Topic: Quantitative Aptitude
Importance: High (2 Marks in GATE AG)

## Key Concepts & Summary
General Aptitude questions in GATE test logical clarity, ratio applications, and standard probability models (Binomial, Poisson, Normal).

## Essential Formulas
- **Binomial Distribution**:
  $$P(X = k) = \binom{n}{k} p^k (1-p)^{n-k}$$
  Mean $\mu = n p$, Variance $\sigma^2 = n p (1-p)$.

- **Poisson Distribution**:
  $$P(X = k) = \frac{\lambda^k e^{-\lambda}}{k!}$$
  Mean $\mu = \lambda$, Variance $\sigma^2 = \lambda$.

- **Work and Time Ratio**:
  $$\text{Work} = \text{Rate} \times \text{Time}$$
  If $A$ takes $T_A$ days and $B$ takes $T_B$ days, together they take:
  $$T_{combined} = \frac{T_A \cdot T_B}{T_A + T_B}$$
